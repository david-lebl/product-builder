# Full Product Catalog & CZK Price List (Sample Data)

> This document reproduces, in full, the sample catalog and CZK price list that the Product Builder application actually loads by default (`SampleCatalog.catalog` + `SamplePricelist.pricelistCzkSheet`). It is a factual reference of every category, material, finish, printing method, and price currently configured in the sample data — not a hypothetical or rounded example.

**Currency:** CZK
**Pricelist version:** `1.0.0-czk-sheet`
**Sheet-priced materials are costed per physical press sheet, 320 × 450 mm (SRA3), with 3 mm bleed and 2 mm gutter between copies on the sheet** — the price shown is per sheet, and the system works out how many finished pieces fit per sheet.

## 7. Volume Discounts

The default price list discounts by **total physical press sheets used in the order** (not simply the item count), since that reflects the actual press run size:

| Total Sheets | Discount Multiplier |
|---|---|
| 1–49 | ×1.00 (no discount) |
| 50–249 | ×0.90 (10% off) |
| 250–999 | ×0.80 (20% off) |
| 1,000+ | ×0.70 (30% off) |

There is no separate quantity-based discount in this price list — materials that aren't sheet-priced (vinyl, PVC banners, roll-up stands, all promotional products) receive no volume discount by default, since no matching tier rule is configured for them.

---

## 8. Manufacturing Speed Surcharges

The price of production speed adjusts by a base multiplier, plus an extra load-based surcharge that increases when the shop's production queue is busier:

| Speed Tier | Base Multiplier | Extra Surcharge as Queue Load Rises |
|---|---|---|
| Express | ×1.35 | +10% at ≥50% load · +15% at ≥70% load · +25% at ≥85% load |
| Standard | ×1.00 | +5% at ≥70% load · +10% at ≥85% load |
| Economy | ×0.85 | none |

## 11. Worked Example Using This Price List

**200× Business Cards, Coated Matte 350gsm, digital printing 4/4, Matte Lamination, Round Corners:**

```
Sheets needed for 200 cards @ 85×55mm on a 320×450mm sheet: (nesting-dependent, illustrative)
Material (Coated Matte 350gsm)         16 CZK/sheet
Ink (Digital 4/4)                       4 CZK/sheet
Matte Lamination                        6 CZK/sheet
Round Corners                        0.50 CZK/sheet
                                    ─────────────────
Per-sheet subtotal                  26.50 CZK/sheet
× number of sheets required for 200 cards
Volume discount tier: 50–249 sheets → ×0.90 (if that many sheets are used)
                                    ─────────────────
+ Setup: Matte Lamination                 300 CZK
+ Setup: Round Corners                     40 CZK
                                    ─────────────────
Total (floor: 200 CZK minimum order)
```

The exact sheet count depends on how many 85×55mm cards nest onto one 320×450mm sheet (accounting for 3mm bleed and 2mm gutter) — see `docs/pricing.md` for the nesting/imposition logic in detail.
